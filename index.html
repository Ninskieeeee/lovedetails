Caesar Cipher Instructions

  1. Type your message in the Cipher Message box.
  2. Enter a shift number.
  3. Click Encipher.
  4. Click Decipher.
  5. Use the same shift number when deciphering.

How Caesar Cipher Works

The Caesar Cipher shifts each letter of your message forward or backward in the alphabet based on the shift number you provide.

Example: If the shift is 3:

  • A becomes D
  • B becomes E
  • C becomes F

When deciphering, the letters are shifted backward using the same number. Non-letter characters like spaces and symbols remain unchanged.
